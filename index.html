How to Save & Use Offline

🍎 On iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom (box with arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  4. CrossSafe now lives on your home screen and opens instantly with zero internet or data needed.

🤖 On Android (Chrome / Brave / Edge)

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots menu (top right) and select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  3. Confirm installation. The app will work completely offline forever.

Safety Note: Hold your phone firmly at chest height facing oncoming vehicles so drivers see the strobe from afar. Always look both ways before stepping onto the roadway.
