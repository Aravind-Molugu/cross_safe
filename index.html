What's New in CrossSafe

About CrossSafe Road Crossing Assistant

CrossSafe is a free, privacy-focused Progressive Web App designed to safeguard pedestrians, night runners, dog walkers, and cyclists when crossing roadways in low-light conditions. CrossSafe transforms your smartphone display into an emergency beacon with high-visibility multi-pulse light strobes, custom text alerts, and haptic feedback.

Emergency Strobe Modes & Hazards

  • Police Strobe: Alternating red and blue multi-burst flashes for high-urgency conspicuity.
  • Split Alternating: Dual-color split screen alternating warning flash.
  • Full Screen Flip: Whole-screen alternating color flip for reflective roadway illumination.
  • Dual Amber Wig-Wag: Circular amber beacon pods emulating school bus and municipal crosswalk hazard flashers.
  • White Beacon: High-lumen pulsing white light for spotlighting and walking visibility.
  • SOS Morse Distress: International optical and audible Morse code distress signaling.

100% Offline & Zero Telemetry

CrossSafe operates completely offline with no cellular data required. It has no advertisements, no tracking cookies, and collects zero personal analytics. All settings and local audit records stay strictly on your device.
